# Foundation Models for PDEs -- Material
Material for the mini course in the Training School of the Special Semester in AI and Scientific Computing at RICAM, Linz in Autumn 2026

The content is inspired by the lecture *AI in the Sciences and Engineering* by Siddhartha Mishra at ETH Zurich.
